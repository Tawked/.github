# Support

- **Help with your integration or your Tawked account**: support@tawked.com, and the docs at [docs.tawked.com](https://docs.tawked.com).
- **Service status**: [tawked.com/en/status](https://tawked.com/en/status).
- **A bug in an SDK**: open an issue in the package's repository. A vulnerability goes through the [security policy](https://github.com/tawked/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) instead.
